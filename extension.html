ONE-TIME SETUP

Unzip. Load. You’re in.

This download installs through your browser’s Developer mode. It is not a browser store listing.

  1. Extract the ZIP

    Keep the packet-party-pocket-search folder somewhere permanent.

  2. Open Extensions

    Chrome: chrome://extensions
    Edge: edge://extensions
    Brave: brave://extensions

  3. Load the folder

    Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, then select the folder containing manifest.json.

  4. Pin & open Packet Party

    Pin it from the puzzle-piece Extensions menu. Click its toolbar icon on a webpage to open your floating search panel.

Small enough to stay out of the way.

Drag the header to move the panel. Minimize it to a floating square and click to reopen. Choose a question bank, show or hide answers, and read explanations at your pace.

Grouped True/False, matching answers and question exhibits are included. Source disagreements stay visible as separate results.

Your search stays with you.

Questions are bundled into the extension, so searching works offline. Your last query and settings are saved locally. Nothing reads your clipboard automatically or scrapes the page.

After navigating, click the toolbar icon to reopen. Browser settings pages and some viewers open search in a separate extension tab instead.
